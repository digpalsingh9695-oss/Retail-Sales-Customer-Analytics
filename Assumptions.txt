RETAIL SALES & CUSTOMER ANALYTICS
PROJECT ASSUMPTIONS AND BUSINESS RULES
============================================================

Project: Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Using SQL
Dataset: Assignment-2 – Retail Analytics
Database Platform: PostgreSQL

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
--------------------
This document records the business rules and analytical assumptions used
while solving the SQL questions in this project. These rules help keep
revenue calculations, order classifications, and customer/product analyses
consistent across queries.

The dataset contains six tables:
- Customers
- Products
- Employees
- Orders
- Order_Items
- Returns

2. REVENUE CALCULATION RULES
----------------------------
2.1 Gross Line-Item Value
Gross value is calculated before applying any discount:

    Gross Value = Quantity × Unit Price at Sale

2.2 Net Line-Item Value
Net value is calculated after applying the line-item discount:

    Net Value = Quantity × Unit Price at Sale × (1 − Discount Rate)

2.3 Revenue Eligibility
Unless a question explicitly states otherwise, revenue calculations include
only orders whose order_status is 'Delivered'.

2.4 Returned and Cancelled Orders
Orders with status 'Returned' or 'Cancelled' are not included in delivered
revenue. Return-related questions may use the Returns table and the gross
value of the returned order items when specifically requested.

3. ORDER AND CUSTOMER ANALYSIS
------------------------------
3.1 Delivered-Order Counts
When a question asks for delivered orders, count only orders with
order_status = 'Delivered'.

3.2 Customers With No Delivered Orders
For questions requiring all customers, customers with zero delivered orders
must remain in the results. A LEFT JOIN or an equivalent approach can be
used where appropriate.

3.3 Repeat Customers
A repeat customer is a customer who has placed at least two delivered
orders, as specified in the assignment.

3.4 Customer Net Spend
Customer net spend is calculated from the net value of order items belonging
to delivered orders, using the discount-adjusted line-item formula above.

4. PRODUCT AND STOCK ANALYSIS
-----------------------------
4.1 Product Revenue
Product revenue is based on the net value of order items from delivered
orders, unless a question specifies a different value.

4.2 Products Without Delivered Sales
Products with no delivered sales should remain eligible for the product
ranking question and should be represented with zero revenue.

4.3 Stock Classification
Products are classified using the assignment's thresholds:
- Low Stock: stock_quantity < 20
- Moderate Stock: stock_quantity between 20 and 40, inclusive
- High Stock: stock_quantity > 40

5. MONTHLY REVENUE AND RUNNING TOTALS
------------------------------------
Monthly order counts and revenue are grouped by year and month. Results are
sorted in chronological order. Running totals accumulate monthly delivered
net revenue from the earliest month to the latest month in the result set.

6. EMPLOYEE ORDER ACTIVITY
--------------------------
For the conditional aggregation question, delivered, returned, and cancelled
orders are counted separately by status. Employees with no orders must also
be included in the output.

7. CUSTOMER REVENUE COMPARISON (QUESTION 14)
--------------------------------------------
The assignment asks for customers whose net revenue is above the average
customer net revenue and requires the scope of the average to be stated.

Assumption used in this project:
The average is calculated across customers with delivered purchases only.
Customers without delivered purchases are excluded from this average because
their delivered net revenue is not included in the comparison population.

8. TOP-CUSTOMER CONTRIBUTION
----------------------------
For the top-three-customer analysis, customer net spend is calculated from
delivered orders after discounts. Each customer's percentage contribution
is calculated against total delivered net revenue.

9. DATA AND IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
--------------------------------
- The supplied dataset and table structure are used for this assignment.
- The project focuses on writing SQL queries; database/table creation and
  data entry are not assessed tasks in the assignment brief.
- The assumptions above are applied only where needed to interpret the
  questions consistently.
- Where a question specifies its own calculation or inclusion rule, that
  question-specific instruction takes precedence.

10. PROJECT TABLES
------------------
Customers    : Customer details, location, and signup date.
Products     : Product category, current price, and stock quantity.
Employees    : Employee details, branch city, and monthly salary.
Orders       : Order dates, customer/employee assignments, status, and
               payment method.
Order_Items  : Products, quantities, sale-time prices, and discount rates.
Returns      : Return dates and reasons for returned orders.
